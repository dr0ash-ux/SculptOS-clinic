## Implementation note
Before production launch in India, SculptOS needs a dedicated legal/compliance review of the Digital Personal Data Protection framework, applicable health-sector requirements, contractual terms of cloud providers, clinical record retention requirements and any state-specific obligations. The product architecture should support these requirements without treating an overseas dental guideline as Indian law.
